Privacy notice

Effective 8 November 2026 · Crow Chinese Reader for iOS and Android

Crow Chinese Reader is operated by Crow Tech Pte. Ltd. This notice covers the reader app and its lesson service. The Crow Tech website privacy policy separately covers visits to crow.sg and messages sent through that website.

Reading on your device

The app keeps downloaded lessons, reading position, saved words, preferences, and study exports on your device. We do not upload that study data to our lesson server. If you choose to export or share it, the destination you choose receives it. Deleting an online account does not erase these local files; removing the app from your device may erase them.

Online account and purchases

Free stories and grammar can be read and downloaded without an account. On iOS, to buy, restore, or download paid stories, the app uses Sign in with Apple. For signed-in users, our server stores Apple's account identifier, an account token used to match purchases, an encrypted Apple authorization refresh token, hashed session tokens, and the public key and counters used to check App Attest. It also stores Apple transaction identifiers and entitlement status needed to grant or restore paid access. We do not receive your payment-card details. The app does not ask Apple to share your name or email address.

On Android, the app does not use a separate sign-in. To buy or restore paid stories, it sends our server the Google Play purchase token for the lifetime purchase, with a Google Play Integrity token used to check the app and device and bind the verification to that request. Our server checks both with Google. It stores the purchase token encrypted, a hash of it, purchase-derived account identifiers, the product, and hashed session tokens. It does not store the Integrity token. The app does not read your Google account name or email address, and we do not receive your payment-card details.

Google Play checks and diagnostics

The Android app includes Google Play Billing. Its built-in reporting sends Google technical app, device and billing-operation diagnostics, such as billing results and errors. This can occur when the app connects to billing or checks available products and purchases, even if you do not buy anything. Google Play Integrity processes request information, app metadata, licensing status and device-attestation information to prevent fraud and unauthorized access. Google handles its service information under its own terms and privacy policy. These checks do not upload your saved words or reading position to Google through our app.

Online requests and service logs

Our service and Cloudflare process your IP address and request details to deliver lessons and limit abuse. Cloudflare may derive your coarse country from your IP address for security analytics; the app does not collect GPS or precise location. Our service does not keep the raw IP address in its account database or access log. Cloudflare's security analytics may retain sampled request records, including source IP, request status and the path of an online lesson you access, for up to 31 days under its current free-plan settings. Apple and Google process sign-in, purchases and Play Integrity checks under their own terms. We do not sell personal data. Opening an external source-credit or dictionary link leaves the app and is subject to that destination's practices.

Our operational service logs contain request method, route template, response status and timing, and fixed verification-failure categories. They do not include purchase or session tokens, account identifiers, raw IP addresses, concrete lesson paths or request bodies. The Cloudflare security records described above are separate from these service logs. The app has no advertising SDK or developer-added usage-analytics SDK; Google Play Billing's built-in diagnostics are described above.

Retention and deletion

Account and purchase-binding records remain while your online account is active. Short-lived sign-in challenges and sessions expire, although expired rows may remain until cleanup. You can delete your online account in Settings. This removes its live server sessions, Apple authorization token, device-key binding and purchase-binding record, and attempts to revoke Apple authorization. Operational backups may retain earlier copies until their rotation; they are restricted to recovery use. On Android, Settings → Delete server purchase data removes the stored purchase binding and its sessions; you can restore the purchase later through Google Play. Apple and Google may retain purchase records under their own policies. Your local study data remains on the device after online account deletion.

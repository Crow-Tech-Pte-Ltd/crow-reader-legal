Crow Chinese Reader support

Read and listen to Chinese stories, save words, and keep your place. A small starter library and grammar chapters work offline. Online downloads require Sign in with Apple. A single optional purchase unlocks the paid story library, including future paid additions as they are released.

Downloads and purchases

Open Settings → Downloads to check or resume lessons. Completed downloads and saved study data remain on the device across normal app updates. Uninstalling the app may remove local downloads and study data, so export important saved words or study data before uninstalling. If you have already purchased the lifetime library, sign in with the same Apple Account and use Settings → Restore purchases. Apple handles payment and refunds.

Account and privacy

Settings includes Delete account. It removes the online account data, while downloaded readings and saved words remain on your device. See the Crow Chinese Reader privacy notice for details.

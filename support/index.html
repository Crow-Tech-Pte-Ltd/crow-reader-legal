Crow Chinese Reader support

Read and listen to Chinese stories, save words, and keep your place. A small starter library and grammar chapters work offline. Free stories and grammar can be downloaded without an account. On iOS, Sign in with Apple is needed only to buy, restore, or download paid stories. Android uses Google Play purchase verification without a separate sign-in. A single optional lifetime purchase on your platform unlocks its paid story library, including future paid additions as they are released.

Downloads and purchases

Open Settings → Downloads to check or resume lessons. Completed downloads and saved study data remain on the device across normal app updates. Uninstalling the app may remove local downloads and study data, so export important saved words or study data before uninstalling. If you have already purchased the lifetime library, sign in with the same Apple Account and use Settings → Restore purchases. Apple handles payment and refunds.

On Android, install the app using the Google Play account that bought the lifetime library, then choose Settings → Restore Google Play purchase. An internet connection and a genuine, supported Google Play installation are needed to verify ownership and download paid stories. Pending or unsuccessful payments do not unlock paid downloads; wait for Google Play to confirm payment and try Restore again. Google Play handles checkout and purchase records; for refunds, use Google Play's order-help options or contact us. An iOS purchase and a Google Play purchase are separate; there is no shared cross-platform account.

Account and privacy

On iOS, Settings includes Delete account. It removes the online account data, while downloaded readings and saved words remain on your device. See the Crow Chinese Reader privacy notice for details.

On Android, Settings → Delete server purchase data removes the live server purchase binding and its sessions. Clear this device's purchase session removes only the local session. Neither action refunds or cancels the Google Play purchase, and Restore can verify ownership again. Local downloads and study data remain until you remove them or uninstall the app. Recovery backups may retain earlier records until rotation.

Contact us

For help, feedback, a refund question, or a privacy or deletion request, email hello@crow.sg or use the Crow Tech contact form. Include Crow Chinese Reader, your platform, app and operating-system versions, and what happened. If you cannot access the app, ask for help deleting the server purchase binding; we may need to verify ownership. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, purchase tokens or sign-in tokens.
